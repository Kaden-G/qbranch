Profile & rules

Health-first training — goal order: health → cardio → joints → agility → aesthetics. The V-taper (lateral delts · upper chest · lats · arms) still matters — just last, not first.

Mission: preserve + build muscle in a physician-supervised GLP-1 deficit (protein ~130–165 g/day is the lever), with cardiovascular health and pain-free joints ranking above the look.
Bodyweight: ~180 lb (2026-09-09).
Block 0 “Show Up”: —
Aesthetics — current priority: Arms are the priority — direct biceps/triceps 3×/week.
Shoulder — chronic, managed ceiling: not an injury in rehab, not on a re-entry ladder to "full." It's a permanent limitation. The payback is delayed 24–72 h, so the pre-committed weekly pressing cap — not in-session pain — is the real guardrail. No overhead triceps extensions.
Knee — flagged, undiagnosed: pain after mild activity. Low-impact only until evaluated; running is held.
Guardrails: shoulder → neutral-grip/landmine pressing + capped weekly pressing volume, no overhead triceps extensions · knee → low-impact only, no running/impact · back → anterior-load + chest-supported · R ankle (Broström) → heel-elevate, cautious single-leg.
Action items: ⏳ VA PT evaluation for knees.
Progression (§8): top of range on all sets at ≤ target RPE → +5 upper / +5–10 lower · reps hit but RPE high → hold · missed / spiking → back off · 2+ bad sessions → deload · load older than 21 days → recalibrate.
RPE ruler: 10 = failure · 9 = 1 RIR · 8 = 2 RIR · 7 = 3 RIR · 6 = 4 RIR.
